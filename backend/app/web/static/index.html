How Lore works (1 minute read)
  1. Import. Your Instagram data export is read: saved posts, collections and links friends shared in chats. The same post saved or shared several times is kept once.
  2. Fetch. For public posts, the video or images are downloaded without using your account (logged out).
  3. Read. Speech is transcribed and on-screen text is read, on this computer.
  4. Understand. An AI turns the caption, on-screen text and speech into a title, summary, topic and lists of places, books, products and tips. Each item is marked ✓ in the post or ~ AI guess.
  5. Search. Everything is searchable here; the original post is always one click away.
